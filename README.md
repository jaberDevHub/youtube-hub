## 💡 What is this?
YouTubeHub turns any YouTube channel into a minimalist, GitHub-style creator card with zero clutter. Just paste a channel link or handle (@MrBeast, @ufc), and get a single-page card built for sharing:

* 🔴 Ambient Red Glow: Full-page atmospheric YouTube red lighting with frosted glassmorphism.
* 📅 12-Month Contribution Heatmap: Signature GitHub-style calendar tracking uploads and streaks.
* 🏆 Play Button Achievements: Circular medal badges from 100K Silver all the way up to 300M Mythic Apex.
* 📊 Live Stats: Real subscriber count, total views, and video uploads.
* 📸 Share Image: One-click download of a high-res, clean screenshot card ready for Twitter, Discord, and LinkedIn.
