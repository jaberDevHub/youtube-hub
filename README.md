## 💡 About YouTubeHub

Traditional YouTube channel pages are cluttered, and standard analytics dashboards are buried behind complex menus. 

**YouTubeHub** reimagines the creator identity with the minimalist, high-impact aesthetic of developer platforms:

* **Instant Generation:** Paste any YouTube handle (`@MrBeast`, `@ufc`), channel URL, or channel ID to automatically generate a verified creator profile.
* **Zero Bloat:** No complicated dashboards, no multi-page navigation, and no unnecessary buttons.
* **Single-Screen Focus:** The entire profile card fits within a single viewport, purpose-built for clean high-resolution social screenshots and sharing.

## 🎨 Features & Highlights

### 🔴 Ambient YouTube Red Aura
* Radiant atmospheric red ambient blur orbs glowing behind the profile card with subtle floating micro-animations.
* Deep graphite canvas paired with frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`) and a laser-sharp red accent border.

### 📅 12-Month Contributions Heatmap
* The signature GitHub-inspired contribution calendar tracking 365 days of video uploads, YouTube Shorts, and publishing milestones.
* Red intensity scaling reflecting content consistency.
* Real-time metrics tracking current publishing streaks, longest recorded streaks, and yearly upload volume.

### 🏆 Play Button Medallion Achievements
* Collectible awards modeled after GitHub profile badges and YouTube's official creator milestones:
  * 🥈 **Silver Play Button** (100K Subscribers)
  * 🥇 **Gold Play Button** (1M Subscribers)
  * 💎 **Diamond Play Button** (10M Subscribers)
  * 🔴 **Custom Ruby Play Button** (50M Subscribers)
  * 💎 **Red Diamond Play Button** (100M Subscribers)
  * 🌌 **Cosmic Play Button** (200M Subscribers)
  * 👑 **Mythic Apex Play Button** (300M Subscribers)

### 📊 Verified Channel Metrics
* Live, accurate statistics directly synchronized with YouTube:
  * Verified Subscriber Count
  * Total Lifetime Video Views
  * Published Catalog Size
  * Verified Creator Badges

### 📸 One-Click Clean Image Export
* Integrated **Share Image** tool renders a clean 2x PNG card without any action buttons or clutter, optimized for sharing across Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Discord, and portfolio sites.
